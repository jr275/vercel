## Scale

Evidence credit per category: 0 none, 0.2 the vendor's own claim, 0.4 independent corroboration (press, tutorial, platform page), 0.8 seen by us, 1.0 seen and excellent. ## Human checklist before award

For each nominee: [ ] three close-ups of faces by the artist who would sculpt Vera (wireframe visible), adult women, at least one aged 40 to 50; [ ] a before and after sculpt, or a design sheet of an original character; [ ] facial animation reel; [ ] one speaking video, one take; [ ] a rigged file with ARKit and visemes, run through the validator; [ ] GLB experience or hand-off plan; [ ] a named team; [ ] disclosure of scans, AI, bases and third-party assets; [ ] rights terms accepted; [ ] two references. A vendor that cannot show the faces is dropped.
